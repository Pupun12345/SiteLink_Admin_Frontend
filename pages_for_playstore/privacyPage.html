SITELINK

Privacy Policy

Learn how SiteLink collects, uses, protects, and manages your personal information while you use our platform.

Last Updated: August 26, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to SiteLink ("SiteLink", "we", "our", or "us"). SiteLink is a digital platform designed to connect construction workers, vendors, contractors, and employers.

Vendors can create business profiles, publish job opportunities, discover workers, and hire workers through the platform. Workers can create professional profiles, discover job opportunities, apply for jobs, and access additional services available through SiteLink.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, process, store, disclose, and protect information relating to users of SiteLink.

By accessing or using SiteLink, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

2. Who This Privacy Policy Applies To

This Privacy Policy applies to users of SiteLink, including:

  • Construction workers and job seekers.
  • Vendors, contractors, employers, and businesses.
  • Users who create worker or vendor profiles.
  • Users who publish or apply for jobs.
  • Users who purchase paid plans or services.
  • Visitors who access SiteLink's website or privacy-policy pages.

3. Information We Collect

SiteLink collects information that is necessary to create and manage user accounts, provide worker and vendor profiles, connect workers with employment opportunities, facilitate job applications and hiring, provide subscriptions and paid services, process payments, maintain platform security, and provide customer support.

The information collected may vary depending on how you use SiteLink.

3.1 Account and Contact Information

When you create or maintain a SiteLink account, we may collect:

  • Full name.
  • Mobile phone number.
  • WhatsApp number, if provided.
  • Email address.
  • Password and authentication information.
  • User type, such as worker or vendor.
  • Account and profile identifiers.

3.2 Worker Profile Information

If you create a worker profile, we may collect:

  • Date of birth.
  • Age.
  • Gender.
  • Profile photograph.
  • Primary skill.
  • Additional skills.
  • Work experience.
  • Experience description.
  • Expected or preferred salary.
  • Salary type, such as daily, weekly, monthly, hourly, or project-based.
  • Work state.
  • City.
  • Work location.
  • Willingness to relocate.
  • Experience certificates.
  • Work sample photographs.
  • Other professional information voluntarily provided by you.

Worker profile information may be used to help vendors discover suitable workers, evaluate applications, and facilitate employment-related services.

3.3 Vendor and Business Information

If you create a vendor, contractor, employer, or business profile, we may collect:

  • Company or business name.
  • Contact information.
  • Designation.
  • Business or work area.
  • Company logo.
  • Business website.
  • GST number.
  • GST certificate.
  • PAN number.
  • PAN card image.
  • Other business or verification information voluntarily submitted by you.

3.4 Government and Verification Documents

Where SiteLink provides identity, business, professional, or verification features, users may be asked to provide government identification information, PAN information, GST information, certificates, or other supporting documents.

These documents may be used for:

  • Identity verification.
  • Business verification.
  • Worker or vendor verification.
  • Fraud prevention.
  • Platform safety.
  • Compliance with applicable legal requirements.
  • Providing verification-related features.
Protection of verification information:

Government identification numbers, PAN information, GST information, and verification documents are treated as confidential and are not intended to be publicly displayed to other SiteLink users.

3.5 Job Posts, Applications, and Hiring Activity

SiteLink allows vendors to publish job opportunities and allows workers to discover and apply for those opportunities.

We may collect information relating to:

  • Jobs posted by vendors.
  • Job descriptions.
  • Job locations.
  • Worker applications.
  • Application status.
  • Hiring activity.
  • Worker and vendor interactions.
  • Job-related communications.
  • Information used during recruitment.

3.6 Payment and Transaction Information

SiteLink provides paid plans and services for workers and vendors.

Vendors may purchase plans or services that provide additional hiring or job-posting capabilities, while workers may purchase plans that provide additional benefits.

When a user purchases a paid plan or service, SiteLink may process information such as:

  • Selected plan.
  • Subscription status.
  • Subscription start and expiration information.
  • Transaction identifiers.
  • Payment identifiers.
  • Payment status.
  • Purchase history.
  • Transaction amount.
  • Other transaction-related information.

Payments are processed through third-party payment service providers, including Razorpay where applicable.

Payment security:

SiteLink does not intentionally store complete debit-card numbers, credit-card numbers, CVV numbers, UPI PINs, or banking authentication credentials on its own servers when such information is processed directly by the applicable payment provider.

3.7 Authentication and Security Information

To authenticate and secure accounts, SiteLink may process:

  • Password information in protected or hashed form.
  • One-time passwords (OTP).
  • OTP expiration information.
  • OTP attempt information.
  • Phone verification status.
  • Account verification status.
  • Firebase authentication identifiers where applicable.

Passwords and OTP information are used for authentication and security purposes and are not intended to be displayed to other users.

3.8 Device and Notification Information

To provide notifications, maintain application functionality, improve reliability, and protect accounts, SiteLink may process:

  • Device type.
  • Device tokens.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) tokens.
  • Firebase authentication identifiers.
  • Application version.
  • Technical and diagnostic information.

Device and notification identifiers may be used to deliver push notifications, maintain application functionality, detect security issues, and troubleshoot technical problems.

3.9 Location Information

SiteLink may process location or location-related information provided by users, such as:

  • State.
  • City.
  • Work location.
  • Job location.
  • Other location information provided by the user.

Location-related information may be used for job discovery, worker availability, recruitment, and employment-related services.

If the SiteLink mobile application requests device location permission, the location obtained through that permission will be used for the relevant SiteLink functionality for which the permission was requested.

Users can manage device location permissions through their device settings.

3.10 Profile Images and Uploaded Files

Users may upload photographs, profile images, company logos, work samples, certificates, PAN-related documents, GST-related documents, and other files supported by SiteLink.

Uploaded files may be stored using third-party cloud or file-storage infrastructure used by SiteLink.

Files containing government identification, tax information, or other sensitive information are intended to be accessible only for authorized SiteLink functionality and should not be publicly exposed.

3.11 Ratings and Verification Information

SiteLink may maintain information relating to worker performance and verification, including:

  • Automatically calculated worker ratings.
  • Number of completed jobs.
  • Number of jobs used to calculate a rating.
  • Rating update dates.
  • Administrative ratings.
  • Administrative rating comments.
  • Verification status.
  • Verification rejection reasons.
  • Verification review dates.

This information may be used to improve trust, recruitment, worker discovery, platform safety, and verification features.

3.12 Information You Publish Through SiteLink

SiteLink provides community-style functionality where users may publish job posts, profile information, descriptions, and other content.

Information that you intentionally make available to other SiteLink users may be visible to those users according to the functionality of the application.

Please do not publicly post sensitive information.

This includes passwords, OTPs, PAN numbers, government identification numbers, banking credentials, UPI PINs, or other confidential information.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, loss, misuse, or destruction.

Depending on the nature of the information and service, security measures may include:

  • Encrypted transmission of information.
  • Authentication and access controls.
  • Secure password handling.
  • Server-side security controls.
  • Restricted access to sensitive information.
  • Security monitoring.
  • Fraud prevention mechanisms.

Passwords are stored using password hashing rather than being stored as plain-text passwords.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Therefore, while we work to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.
